Slow Story English Publisher

Internal publishing tool for the YouTube channel Slow Story English.

What it is

Slow Story English Publisher is a command-line tool used by the owner of the Slow Story English channel to publish the channel's own videos: it uploads a finished video file, sets its title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles and schedule, and adds it to a playlist. It also updates the channel's description, keywords and banner.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not distributed to the public and does not accept other users' accounts.

How it uses the YouTube API

  • Uploads videos to the owner's channel (videos.insert) and sets thumbnails and captions.
  • Updates the owner's channel branding (channels.update, channelBanners.insert).
  • Creates playlists and adds the uploaded videos to them.

It never reads, downloads or modifies content from other channels.
